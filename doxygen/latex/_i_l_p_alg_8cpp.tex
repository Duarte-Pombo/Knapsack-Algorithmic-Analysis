vector$<$ \mbox{\hyperlink{class_pallet}{Pallet}} $>$ ILPAlgorithm (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{\mbox{\hyperlink{class_truck}{Truck}} \&}]{truck}{, }\item[{vector$<$ \mbox{\hyperlink{class_pallet}{Pallet}} $>$ \&}]{pallets}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Solves the 0/1 knapsack problem using a branch and bound algorithm tailored for truck loading with pallet constraints. 

Pallets are selected to maximize profit while respecting the truck’s weight limit. Ties are broken by fewer pallets and lower ID sum.


\begin{DoxyParams}{Parameters}
{\em truck} & The truck object with a max weight limit. \\
\hline
{\em pallets} & The list of available pallets with weight and value. \\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
\begin{DoxyReturn}{Returns}
A vector of selected pallets forming the optimal solution.
\end{DoxyReturn}
{\bfseries{Complexity\+:}} O(2\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}n) in the worst case (due to branching), but pruning and bounding significantly reduce this in practice. Sorting takes O(n log n).
